Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Notaru has no account, no server and no tracker. Your recordings, your notes and your key stay on your phone.

The only thing that leaves the device is the audio you record, sent to the transcription service you configured yourself, with your own key, followed by the resulting text so that notes can be written from it.

We — the author of the app — receive nothing. No data ever reaches us.

Who publishes this app

Notaru is developed and published by an independent developer. For any question about this policy or about your data: cntctari@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

DataWhere it lives
Audio recordingsThe app's private storage, deleted as they go (see below)
Transcripts and notesThe app's private storage
Your API keyEncrypted by Android's keystore (EncryptedSharedPreferences)
Your preferencesThe app's private storage

Android's automatic backup is disabled for this app. Nothing is copied to Google Drive or transferred to a new device without your knowledge. Moving your data is an explicit action from the app's settings, and the file it produces never contains your key.

What is sent, to whom, and why

Transcription and note writing need computing power a phone does not have. They are therefore handed to an external service, which you pick in the settings and for which you supply your own key:

  • Groq (default) — privacy policy
  • OpenAI (optional) — privacy policy

Two things are sent: the audio chunks, to be transcribed, then the text of the transcript, to produce a summary and an outline. Nothing else leaves the device — no identifier, no location, no contacts, no device information.

The contractual relationship for those transfers is between you and that service, since it is your key and your account that are used. As of 21 September 2026, Groq states that it does not train its models on data sent to its API and does not retain it by default. Those policies are theirs and may change: the links above are authoritative, not this page.

Deletion of audio chunks

Each audio chunk is deleted from the phone as soon as its transcription succeeds. If it fails — no network, service unavailable — the chunk is kept and retried later, including after the app restarts: deleting it would permanently lose that passage. It disappears as soon as the transcription succeeds.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-up, no identity.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting.
  • No advertising, no ad tracker, no advertising identifier.
  • No third-party SDK collecting data.
  • No sale or sharing of data for commercial purposes.
  • No listening outside the recordings you start yourself.

Age requirement

Notaru is intended for people aged 18 and over. This is not a preference: the app only works with a key issued by Groq or OpenAI, and both services require account holders to be 18 or older.

We knowingly collect no data about minors. Since no data reaches us at all, no profile is built for any user.

Deleting your data

Everything is on your phone, so everything goes with it:

  • Settings → Delete all my data erases recordings, notes and key from this device, immediately and irreversibly.
  • Uninstalling the app has the same effect.

We have nothing to delete on our side: we hold nothing. For data already sent to Groq or OpenAI, the request goes to that service, where your own account is involved.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new update date. A change affecting what leaves the device will also be announced inside the app.
